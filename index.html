SUPPLY GUIDE · KENYA

How to Buy Laptops, Medical Supplies and Uniforms Online in Kenya Without Meeting a Supplier

You can arrange many business and institutional purchases remotely, provided the requirements, supplier terms, payment details and delivery arrangements are checked before you commit.

1. Start with a clear item request

Describe the goods you need as precisely as possible. For laptops, include the preferred brand or model, processor, memory, storage, screen size and whether new or refurbished equipment is acceptable. For medical equipment and consumables, identify the intended use, product type, specifications, quantities and any applicable regulatory or facility requirements. For uniforms, provide garment type, sizes, fabric preferences, colours and any branding requirements. A clear request reduces misunderstandings and helps the quotation reflect the actual need.

2. Ask for a written digital quotation

Before paying, request a quotation or pro-forma invoice that identifies the item, specifications, quantity, unit and total costs, delivery charges where applicable, expected fulfilment timeline, payment instructions and any warranty or return terms that apply. Do not rely on a product name alone where different models or specifications may have very different prices. For regulated goods, ask what product documentation or approvals are relevant to the specific item and intended use.

3. Confirm supplier and product details

Check that the description on the quotation matches what you requested. For technology, confirm the exact model and whether the equipment is new or refurbished, together with warranty details where offered. For medical equipment, consumables and PPE, confirm the product identity, intended use, labelling and any applicable Kenyan regulatory requirements. For uniforms and branded workwear, confirm sizes, samples or artwork approvals, quantities and production details before authorising the order.

4. Use clear, traceable payment arrangements

Once the quotation and terms have been confirmed, use the agreed payment method, such as M-Pesa or bank transfer. Verify payment details through an established communication channel before sending funds, and keep the quotation, payment confirmation and receipt. Where pay-on-confirmation arrangements are offered, make sure the exact conditions are recorded in writing. Never share PINs, passwords or one-time verification codes as part of a purchase enquiry.

5. Agree on delivery and keep the records

Confirm the delivery town, recipient details, courier or shuttle arrangement, estimated dispatch and arrival windows, tracking or waybill information, and how receipt will be acknowledged. For institutional orders, agree who is authorised to receive the goods and what delivery documentation is required. Keep the digital quotation, correspondence, payment record, tracking details and e-receipt together for your procurement records.
