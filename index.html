SERVICE GUIDE

Understand each service
before you book.

Open any service to review what it includes, the advertised fee or quotation basis, an estimated turnaround, and what to prepare. Timelines begin once the brief, required information and agreed payment are received.

01 Company Registration KES 15,000 shown on the service card +

Support with preparing and submitting a company-registration application through the relevant official process. We first confirm the proposed business name, ownership structure and type of entity before preparing the application.

Price structure

KES 15,000 shown on the service card. Before work starts, confirm in writing what this fee covers, whether official government charges are included, and whether any name-search, filing or third-party costs are additional.

Estimated delivery

About 3–7 working days for our preparation once all information is supplied. Official approval may take longer and is controlled by the relevant authority.

What we need from you

Proposed company names; directors’/shareholders’ full details and identification information; contact details; ownership/share allocation; registered office details; and any forms or declarations required by the official process.

Enquire about this service ↗
02 Company Profile Development KES 10,000 shown on the service card +

A professional company profile that explains who you are, what you do, your experience, strengths and contact details. It can be prepared for customers, partners, funding conversations or supplier introductions.

Price structure

KES 10,000 shown on the service card. Confirm the agreed length, design level, number of revisions and whether printing or extra language versions cost extra.

Estimated delivery

About 3–5 working days after receiving the company information and approving the outline.

What we need from you

Company name and logo (if available); background and mission; services/products; target customers; achievements or references; team details; contact information; brand colours and any existing profile.

Enquire about this service ↗
03 Company Documentation & Building Request a quote +

Help identifying, drafting and organising a practical set of business documents, such as internal forms, registers, standard letters and operating documents. The exact bundle is agreed before work begins.

Price structure

Request a quote. Price depends on the number, type, length and complexity of documents. The quotation should list each document, any bundle discount, revisions and any external professional fees separately.

Estimated delivery

About 5–15 working days for a small-to-medium document pack; larger packs are scheduled after scoping.

What we need from you

Business overview; list of documents needed; existing templates; current procedures; intended users; approval/sign-off person; any format or regulatory requirements.

Enquire about this service ↗
04 Policy Writing KES 5,000 shown on the service card +

Drafting clear workplace or operational policies that explain expected behaviour, responsibilities, procedures and how issues are handled. Examples may include HR, data handling, customer service or workplace conduct policies.

Price structure

KES 5,000 shown on the service card. Confirm whether this is per policy, the expected length, revision allowance and whether legal review is excluded or separately arranged.

Estimated delivery

About 3–5 working days per straightforward policy after the discovery questions are answered; complex policies take longer.

What we need from you

Organisation details; purpose of the policy; staff/team structure; current practices; applicable internal rules; relevant templates; and the person who will approve the final draft. Regulated/legal compliance should be reviewed by a qualified professional.

Enquire about this service ↗
05 Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) KES 4,000 shown on the service card +

A written document setting out the purpose of a proposed collaboration, each party’s responsibilities, contributions, timelines, governance and how the parties will handle changes or disagreements.

Price structure

KES 4,000 shown on the service card. Confirm whether the fee covers one draft, review rounds and formatting. Independent legal advice or negotiation support may cost extra.

Estimated delivery

About 2–4 working days after all parties’ details and agreed terms are received.

What we need from you

Legal names and contacts of all parties; collaboration purpose; each party’s duties/contributions; duration; costs or resource commitments; confidentiality/IP expectations; exit/change arrangements; and signatories. Have a qualified lawyer review it where legally consequential.

Enquire about this service ↗
06 Affidavits & Sworn Statements KES 2,000 shown on the service card +

Help organising the facts and preparing a draft statement in a clear format for the relevant authorised person to administer or commission. We do not witness or swear the statement ourselves unless separately authorised by law.

Price structure

KES 2,000 shown on the service card. Confirm whether this is drafting only. Commissioner/notary charges, stamps or other official costs should be clarified before proceeding.

Estimated delivery

Usually 1–2 working days for drafting after facts and purpose are confirmed. Signing/sworn administration depends on the authorised officer’s availability.

What we need from you

Purpose and receiving institution; accurate chronology/facts; full names and identification details as required; supporting records; and any prescribed format. The person making the statement must check its accuracy and swear/sign it before an authorised officer.

Enquire about this service ↗
07 Consent & Authorization Letters KES 1,500 shown on the service card +

A formal letter recording permission or authorising a named person to perform a specified task, collect an item, represent someone or access information, depending on the receiving organisation’s requirements.

Price structure

KES 1,500 shown on the service card. Confirm whether supporting forms, certified copies, translation or special formatting are included.

Estimated delivery

Usually 1 working day after the correct names, purpose and recipient requirements are supplied.

What we need from you

Name and contact of the authorising person; name and identification details of the authorised person; exact permitted action; recipient organisation; validity dates if applicable; and any prescribed form or supporting documents.

Enquire about this service ↗
08 Proposals — All Types Request a quote +

A structured proposal that explains a need or opportunity, objectives, proposed activities, deliverables, schedule, budget and why the plan should be supported. This may be a business, project, partnership or funding proposal.

Price structure

Request a quote. Pricing depends on length, research, budget development, complexity and deadline. The quote should specify sections included, research limits and revision rounds.

Estimated delivery

About 4–10 working days after the brief and source material are complete; research-heavy proposals may take longer.

What we need from you

Purpose and audience; problem/opportunity; objectives; planned activities; beneficiaries/customers; delivery team; budget assumptions; timeline; evidence or references; and any funder/client template or evaluation criteria.

Enquire about this service ↗
09 Tender Documentation Request a quote +

Assistance organising and checking a tender response against the published instructions, including a compliance checklist and preparation of the agreed narrative or supporting documents. The client remains responsible for the accuracy and final submission unless separately agreed.

Price structure

Request a quote. Price depends on tender size, number of forms, technical complexity and time remaining. Government fees, bid security, taxes and other third-party costs are not assumed included; agree these separately.

Estimated delivery

Typically 3–10 working days depending on tender complexity and deadline. Only accept work when there is enough time to review requirements carefully.

What we need from you

Complete tender notice and documents; eligibility requirements; company registration and tax/compliance records as requested; technical approach; pricing information; relevant experience; required forms; signatory details; and deadline. Never provide false credentials or unsupported claims.

Enquire about this service ↗
10 Letters of Appeal KES 2,000 shown on the service card +

A formal appeal explaining the decision being challenged, relevant facts, reasons for reconsideration and the specific outcome requested. The tone and evidence are tailored to the receiving institution.

Price structure

KES 2,000 shown on the service card. Confirm whether evidence review, attachments, urgent turnaround or additional appeal stages are included.

Estimated delivery

Usually 1–2 working days after receiving the decision and full background. Urgent deadlines must be discussed before payment.

What we need from you

Copy of the decision/notice; date and appeal deadline; relevant case/reference number; factual timeline; grounds for appeal; supporting evidence; recipient details; and the result being requested.

Enquire about this service ↗
11 Demand Letters KES 2,500 shown on the service card +

A formal letter setting out the issue, relevant dates, what action or payment is requested and a reasonable response deadline, based on the client’s instructions. It is not a guarantee of recovery or a substitute for legal representation.

Price structure

KES 2,500 shown on the service card. Confirm whether document review, attachments, delivery/dispatch and any lawyer’s review are included or charged separately.

Estimated delivery

Usually 1–2 working days after the facts and supporting documents are reviewed.

What we need from you

Names and contact details of the parties; clear account of the issue; agreement/invoice/receipts or other evidence; amounts and dates where relevant; prior communication; desired remedy; and recipient address. Obtain legal advice for complex disputes or formal proceedings.

Enquire about this service ↗
12 Recommendation Letters KES 1,500 shown on the service card +

A professionally structured letter describing the person’s or organisation’s relevant strengths, achievements and suitability for a specific opportunity, based on truthful information supplied by the recommender.

Price structure

KES 1,500 shown on the service card. Confirm whether one draft, revisions, letterhead formatting and urgent delivery are included.

Estimated delivery

Usually 1 working day after the purpose and facts are supplied.

What we need from you

Name of the person being recommended; opportunity and recipient; relationship to recommender; genuine achievements or examples; relevant skills; recommender’s correct details and authority; and any required format.

Enquire about this service ↗
13 Cover Letters KES 1,000 shown on the service card +

A concise, tailored letter introducing an application, proposal, tender or submission and highlighting the most relevant information for the recipient.

Price structure

KES 1,000 shown on the service card. Confirm whether tailoring to multiple roles/recipients, revisions or matching CV/proposal edits are included.

Estimated delivery

Usually 1 working day after receiving the target opportunity and supporting document.

Job/opportunity advert or submission instructions; CV/profile or proposal being introduced; recipient details if known; relevant experience; key message; and any word/page limit.

14 E-Related Services Request a quote +

A discovery-led category for digital or online support. The exact service could involve online business support or market-analysis-related work; we should define the specific output before pricing instead of treating all digital tasks as one product.

Price structure

Request a quote. Fee depends on the exact deliverable, research/data access, tools, reporting depth and ongoing support. Any subscriptions or third-party platform costs should be identified separately.

Estimated delivery

Small defined tasks may take 2–5 working days; analysis or ongoing work requires a scoped schedule and agreed reporting dates.

Price structure

Request a quote. Fee depends on word count, research level, editing versus drafting support, references and deadline. Agree scope and originality expectations before work begins.

About 2–7 working days for a short speech or document; longer or research-intensive work needs a separate schedule.
